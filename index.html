What Is Duckpin?

Bowling, Scaled Down

Duckpin is played on a bowling lane with a smaller ball and short, squat pins called duckpins. It’s casual, quick to learn, and a lot of laughs.

Easy for Little Hands

The balls are light enough for the youngest bowlers to roll on their own.

Quick to Learn

No experience needed. Most people get the hang of it in a frame or two.

Made for Groups

A fun change of pace for parties, team outings, and family nights.

A non-refundable 50% deposit holds your date. Book at least 1 week ahead, or 2 weeks for All-Star and MVP.

Our Story

The Mowery Family Story

Midway has been in the Mowery family since the day it opened. Dan Mowery’s grandfather started it to give Carlisle a place for family fun, and that’s still the whole idea.

  1. 1956Midway opens on Holly Pike with 16 lanes.
  2. 1957Bowling takes off, and ten more lanes go in.
  3. 1971Ten more lanes bring Midway to 36.
  4. 1991The Sparetime Lounge opens with food and drinks.
  5. 2015Dan Mowery, the third generation, takes over and starts renovating.
  6. 202670 years in: bowling, duckpin, axe throwing, an arcade, and the patio.

Here for the Next 70

Dan plans to keep doing what his grandfather started: creating family fun. Come make some memories of your own.
